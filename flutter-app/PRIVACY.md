# Auction Arena privacy notice

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Auction Arena displays cricket auction information supplied by tournament organizers, including player names, photographs, playing roles, cricket statistics, bids and team assignments.

The mobile client does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or device storage. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics in this release. Network requests are sent to the configured Auction Arena service so the app can retrieve auctions, player images, live bids and squads. Standard server logs may contain technical information such as IP address, request time, device/browser identifiers and error details for security and reliability.

Tournament organizers are responsible for having permission to publish player information and photographs. Players may request correction or removal through their tournament organizer. Data retention and account deletion requests are handled by the organizer operating the relevant auction.

This notice must be updated before adding organizer accounts, payments, analytics, advertising, push notifications or any new personal-data collection.
