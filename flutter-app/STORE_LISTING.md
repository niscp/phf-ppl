## App name

Auction Arena

## Short description

Follow live cricket auctions, player stats, bids, purses and team squads.

## Full description

Auction Arena brings the energy of a live cricket player auction to your phone.

Follow the player currently under the hammer, the active bid and bidding team, team purse and maximum-bid information, and completed squads as the auction unfolds. Browse registered players with their photos, roles, batting figures and bowling figures. Join a published auction by ID and move between multiple events without reinstalling the app.

Built for cricket communities and tournament organizers, Auction Arena keeps spectators, captains and players on the same live board.

Key features:

- Live cricket auction board
- Player photos, roles and performance statistics
- Team purse, bidding and squad views
- Searchable player pool
- Multiple auctions via auction ID
- Automatic live refresh with network recovery

Auction availability and data are controlled by the tournament organizer.
